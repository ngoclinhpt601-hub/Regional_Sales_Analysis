# 📊 USA Regional Sales Analysis 📈

> Sales Performance, Profitability, and Regional Growth Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Acme Co.'s sales dataset spanning 2014–2018 across the United States to uncover revenue drivers, profit performance, customer behaviors, and market opportunities. The analysis focuses on understanding how sales vary by product, channel, region, and state while identifying seasonal patterns, margin trends, and outlier transactions.

The objective is not only to explore the data, but to translate findings into actionable business recommendations that support pricing strategy, channel optimization, regional expansion, and stronger customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Acme Co. needed a clearer understanding of:

- Which products and customer groups generate the most revenue
- Which sales channels are most effective and scalable
- Which regions and states show the strongest performance and where growth opportunities remain
- How seasonality and pricing influence revenue and profitability
- Whether customer concentration and outlier transactions create strategic risks

The analysis was designed to support better decision-making in sales planning, demand forecasting, and profit maximization.

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Revenue and Seasonality
- Sales showed a stable year-over-year pattern with recurring seasonal peaks and troughs.
- The strongest revenue periods occurred in late spring to early summer, while January consistently marked a low point.
- This suggests an identifiable seasonal demand cycle that can inform planning, promotions, and inventory scheduling.

### 2. Product Performance
- Products 26 and 25 delivered the highest total revenue, making them core revenue drivers.
- Several mid-tier products still presented significant growth potential.
- The biggest opportunity is to improve performance among lower-tier products through pricing, promotions, and product mix optimization.

### 3. Channel Mix
- Wholesale dominated sales contribution, followed by Distributors and Exports.
- Profit margins remained relatively consistent across channels, indicating that each route is operationally strong.
- Export channel showed the highest average margin, suggesting a promising area for expansion.

### 4. Regional Performance
- The West region led total sales, followed closely by the South and Midwest.
- The Northeast trailed behind and represents a meaningful opportunity for targeted market expansion.
- Regional analysis highlights the need for more focused investment in underperforming territories.

### 5. State-Level Insights
- California was the strongest state by revenue and order volume.
- Illinois, Florida, and Texas formed a strong secondary cluster.
- States with lower order volume may benefit from promotional campaigns, channel partnership strategies, and localized market activation.

### 6. Customer Behavior and Concentration Risk
- A small group of customers contributed a disproportionate share of revenue.
- The top customers showed strong revenue generation, but this concentration also creates risk if relationships weaken or demand shifts.
- A balanced strategy should focus on protecting key accounts while growing the lower-revenue portfolio.

### 7. Profitability Drivers
- Revenue and profit were strongly correlated, indicating that growth remains the key value driver.
- Unit price had a major influence on both revenue and profitability.
- Quantity alone was not the main driver; pricing strategy and customer mix were more impactful.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Optimize Pricing Strategy
   - Use pricing insights to improve margin-heavy product mix without sacrificing volume.
   - Focus on premium and high-performing products while reviewing underperforming SKUs.

2. Strengthen Growth in Underpenetrated Regions
   - Increase investment in the Northeast and other lower-performing regions through localized campaigns and strategic partnerships.

3. Expand Export Channel Opportunities
   - The export channel showed strong margins and could be a strategic growth lever for diversifying revenue streams.

4. Improve Customer Portfolio Balance
   - Protect top customers while designing targeted upsell and cross-sell programs for mid-tier accounts.

5. Support Seasonal Planning
   - Use demand seasonality to schedule promotions and inventory planning more effectively.

6. Monitor Outlier Transactions
   - Evaluate extreme pricing events and promotional outliers to determine whether they are strategic or should be normalized.
